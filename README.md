# From Ground to Journey

A scroll-controlled, real-footage construction film of the Flood Road interchange on the Bruce Highway in Queensland, Australia.

Open [the live site](https://iamstarter.github.io/motorway-scroll-film/) and scroll down to move forward through construction. Scroll up to reverse. The site uses 624 locally hosted WebP frames and no JavaScript dependencies.

## Footage and license

Footage: **TMRQld**, [Time lapse of Flood Road interchange construction (Bruce Highway, Cooroy to Curra, Section D)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Time_lapse_of_Flood_Road_interchange_construction_(Bruce_Highway,_Cooroy_to_Curra,_Section_D).webm), licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Changes: trimmed the opening and ending, extracted 624 frames, compressed them to WebP, and built the scroll-controlled website with text and styling.

The source spans about 18 months but contains scene and weather cuts. This is real footage of an interchange, not an uninterrupted recording of a complete motorway.
